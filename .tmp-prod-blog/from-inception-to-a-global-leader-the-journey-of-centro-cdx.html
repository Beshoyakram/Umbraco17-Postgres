From Inception to a Global Leader: The Journey of Centro

  • BPO
  • 587 Views

From a bold vision in 2009 to a global powerhouse in 2025, Centro has transformed the landscape of Business Process Outsourcing (BPO) and Customer Experience (CX) solutions. Founded by industry pioneer Hesham Farag in Fairfax, Virginia, Centro was built on the belief that outsourcing should be more than just a cost-saving tactic; it should be a strategic advantage that elevates brands, enhances customer journeys, and fuels business growth.

The Centro Story

From Inception to a Global Outsourcing Leader.

What began with a vision in Virginia in 2009 has grown into a global outsourcing organization built around talent, operational excellence, technology and long-term client partnerships.

USA Egypt Philippines UAE & KSA
Growing Since 2009
Where It Started

Built on the belief that outsourcing should create strategic value.

Centro was founded in 2009 by Hesham Farag in Fairfax, Virginia, with a vision that went beyond traditional cost-focused outsourcing.

The idea was to build operations that could strengthen customer experiences, create access to global talent and help businesses scale with greater flexibility.

2009 The journey begins
2026 Global delivery continues
The Journey

Growth built one milestone at a time.

Centro’s development has combined geographic expansion with new capabilities, technology and an increasingly sophisticated global delivery model.

2009
Foundation

Centro is founded in Virginia.

Centro begins operations in Fairfax, Virginia, establishing the foundation for what would become a global outsourcing delivery organization.

2010
International Expansion

Cairo expands the talent model.

Just one year later, Centro expands into Cairo, opening access to a large multilingual talent pool and creating a new international delivery capability.

2015
Asia-Pacific Delivery

Manila strengthens around-the-clock delivery.

Centro expands into the Philippines, strengthening its ability to support clients across time zones and broaden its global talent base.

2016
U.S. Growth

Winchester adds another U.S. delivery operation.

Centro opens a facility in Winchester, Virginia, strengthening its domestic capabilities while the international delivery network continues to expand.

2021
Capability Expansion

The business evolves beyond traditional BPO.

Centro broadens its capabilities into digital transformation, data intelligence and end-to-end customer experience, reflecting the changing needs of modern enterprises.

2022
Experience Innovation

El Gouna becomes a new CX delivery hub.

Centro opens a Customer Experience Center in El Gouna, Egypt, adding a new delivery environment designed around modern customer experience operations.

2023
Middle East Expansion

Dubai and Riyadh extend Centro’s regional presence.

Expansion into the UAE and Saudi Arabia creates new opportunities to support businesses operating across some of the region’s fastest-growing markets.

2025
Industry Recognition

Three consecutive years in the IAOP Global Outsourcing 100.

Centro receives its third consecutive IAOP Global Outsourcing 100 recognition, marking another step in the company’s growing international industry profile.

2026
Continuing Momentum

Centro earns IAOP recognition for a fourth consecutive year.

Centro is named to the 2026 IAOP Global Outsourcing 100 as a Rising Star, extending its recognition to four consecutive years and reinforcing its continued momentum in the global outsourcing market.

More Than Geographic Growth

Expansion has been accompanied by a broader operating model that connects global talent, technology and execution across different customer and business processes.

What Comes Next

The next chapter is still being built.

Centro’s journey has never been defined by expansion alone. Each stage has added new talent, capabilities, delivery options and expertise to the organization.

As customer expectations and outsourcing models continue to evolve, Centro’s focus remains on scalable, tech-enabled solutions, operational excellence and measurable outcomes for clients across industries and markets.
