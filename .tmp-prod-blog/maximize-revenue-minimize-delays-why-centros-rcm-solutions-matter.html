The Revenue Challenge

What Healthcare Providers Are Navigating

Complex payer requirements, evolving regulations, claim denials, and coding errors can slow reimbursement and create avoidable revenue leakage. The industry benchmarks below show how widespread the challenge has become.

~12%

Initial Claim Denial Rate

The average initial denial rate was reported at nearly 12% in 2023, compared with 10.15% in 2020.

Simbo.ai, 2023
38%

Providers Reporting Frequent Denials

More than one-third of providers reported that at least one in every ten claims is denied.

Experian, 2024

Coding and Requirement Gaps

Incorrect coding and missed insurance requirements can contribute significantly to delayed or lost revenue.

Advisory Board, 2021
